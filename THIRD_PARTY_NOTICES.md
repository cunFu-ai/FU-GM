## Nortantis

FU-GM vendors Nortantis from
[jeheydorn/nortantis](https://github.com/jeheydorn/nortantis) at upstream commit
`16d81ce92b474b9c61b86877b57b0a255832fdc1`.

Nortantis is licensed under the GNU Affero General Public License version 3.
The complete license is retained at `integrations/nortantis/LICENSE`, and the
corresponding modified source is included in `integrations/nortantis`.

FU-GM modifications made between 2026-06-23 and 2026-08-25 include:

- a headless JSON-to-map exporter at
  `integrations/nortantis/src/nortantis/tools/FuGmHeadlessExporter.java`;
- a small icon rendering adjustment in
  `integrations/nortantis/src/nortantis/IconDrawer.java`;
- longer Gradle download timeouts and a different official Gradle distribution
  host;
- FU-GM integration examples and documentation.

Nortantis' bundled libraries, artwork, dictionaries, and other inherited assets
retain their upstream notices. See `integrations/nortantis/README.txt` and the
notices within the vendored tree.

FU-GM invokes Nortantis as a separate Java map-rendering process. FU-GM's Python
world graph remains the authoritative rules state; the rendered map is a visual
artifact.
